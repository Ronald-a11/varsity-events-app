How {{ SITE_NAME }} works

University events in Zimbabwe live in WhatsApp groups, faded notice-board posters and statuses that expire in 24 hours. This puts them all in one place — every university, searchable, ticketed, and honest about whether tickets are still going.

For students

  1. 1

    Sign in and see everything

    Tell us your university and what you're into. You land on a feed of what's on right now — your own campus first, every other Zimbabwean university right behind it.

  2. 2

    See where to buy — or that it's gone

    Every event lists exactly where tickets are sold: the SRC offices, a partner outlet, EcoCash, the door. Each outlet shows whether it still has stock, and anything that's gone is marked SOLD OUT or not currently available — no wasted trips.

  3. 3

    Show up

    Add it to your calendar, then flash your ticket at the door.
